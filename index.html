Our Story

About Akira

Tradition Meets Excellence

Founded by M. Chethana Peters in the heart of Payagala, Sri Lanka, Akira Incense Products represents the perfect harmony between ancient traditions and modern excellence. Our journey began with a simple mission: to create incense sticks that not only fragrance your space but elevate your spiritual practice.

Each stick is hand-rolled using time-honored techniques passed down through generations, combined with carefully selected natural ingredients sourced from the lush landscapes of Sri Lanka. We believe in the power of scent to transform spaces and minds.
